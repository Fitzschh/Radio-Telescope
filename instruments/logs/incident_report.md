# Incident Report (October 25, 2026 1:02PM)

## Scientific Instrument: Radio Telescope Tip Over

Reason for incident is still unknown, the instrument appears to be fine, no visible nor major damages 
and might still operate as intended. Relocation and fortification  of the instrument's  position  and 
foundation is crucial. Therefore,  additional parts is required to stabilize the  instrument to avoid 
such incidents occuring again. 

## Action Plan

Relocation for the  instrument has been decided to be done on a flat and stable surface. This prevents 
the incident occur again by changing the area to a flat one instead of a slope. To  be able to  do so,
the following materials are needed:

1 x 20m RG6 Coaxial Cable
1 x 5-6m Metal Pole
1 x Bag of Cement Powder

The initial structure is designed for portabilitiy and only serves as a temporary  solution to continue
scientific recordings. Another  design is reserved  for  a  scientific instrument that  is scalable and
automated.
